Our philosophy

Manage relationships. Not transactions.

We believe selling forms lasting partnerships when the process is thoughtful, personalized, and transparent. Successful companies know the winning formula:

Transparency → Trust → Relationships → Currency
The problem

Deal complexity outpaces process long before the infrastructure catches up.

You don't have an ops team, a forecasting tool, or a champion who knows how to sell internally on your behalf. The deal has to survive on its own between calls, and mostly, it doesn't.

Every deal lives in a different inbox

Notes in one thread, the pricing doc in another, the contract somewhere in Drive. By the time a prospect asks "where were we?", nobody has a clean answer.

Your champion is on their own once the call ends

The real buying decision happens in a room you're not in. Your champion walks in without the business case, the numbers, or the talking points to make it stick.

You find out a deal's dead after it's dead

No CRM field tells you a stakeholder went quiet three weeks ago. It just shows up as closed-lost, and by then there's nothing left to do about it.

New reps take months to sound like your best one

Your top rep has a system in their head. Everyone else is improvising. There's no shared playbook that travels with the deal, not the person.

Five things, done well

Everything a deal needs, nothing it doesn't.

01

Deal rooms, built automatically

After every call, myBivy turns your notes into a shareable, buyer-facing room, summary, open action items, and content, organized and ready to send in minutes.

02

Know your buying committee

myBivy maps who's actually involved in the decision, surfaces the people you haven't met yet, and tells you who's gone quiet before it costs you the deal.

03

Shared next steps, not a static PDF

A mutual action plan both sides can see, with named owners and dates. It updates as the deal moves, so nobody's guessing what happens next or who owns it.

04

See risk before it's too late

Real engagement signals: who opened what, when, and for how long. A deal going cold shows up as a flag weeks before it shows up as a loss.

05

Give your buyer their own AI

Buyers get an assistant inside the room, to ask questions, get a quick summary, or move things forward on their own time, without waiting on you to reply.

Where the name comes from

Solid. Reinforced. Equalized. No Extension.

Climbers use this checklist to judge whether an anchor is actually safe to trust with a life. We build myBivy against the same four principles, just applied to a deal instead of a rope.

S

Solid

A foundation that actually holds. Real persistence, real security, nothing built on a hope that the data's still there tomorrow.

R

Reinforced

Nothing depends on one person remembering. If a rep is out, the deal room still tells the whole story on its own.

E

Equalized

Buyer and seller see the same shared picture. No one stakeholder is quietly carrying more weight than they should.

NE

No Extension

A deal never moves further than it's actually ready to. No shock-loading a buyer who isn't there yet.
