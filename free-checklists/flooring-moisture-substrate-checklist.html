What gets missed most

  • Installing over concrete without a moisture test.
  • Material that wasn't acclimated.
  • A subfloor out of flat tolerance.
  • Readings taken but never written down.

This is a field checklist, not the code. Your inspector enforces the code and local amendments your jurisdiction has adopted.

Questions trades ask

How do you test concrete moisture before flooring?

Common methods are relative humidity probes in the slab and calcium chloride (MVER) tests. Use the method and limits the flooring manufacturer requires.

Why record moisture readings?

If the floor fails, the first question is whether the slab was too wet. Recorded readings answer it.

What does acclimated mean for flooring?

The material sat in the room's conditions for as long as the manufacturer requires before install.
