Who it's for

Trade owners and foremen working for GCs and homeowners.

If you're tired of paper checklists, unsigned change work and texting photos one at a time, this is for you.

The checklist is on a clipboard

Inspection prep lives on paper, and a missed item means a failed inspection and a second trip.

Extra work goes unsigned

Change work done on a handshake turns into an argument at billing time, and you eat the cost.

Photos go out one text at a time

Proof of your work is scattered across text threads, with no record of what the GC actually got.

Your paperwork doesn't match your work

GCs remember the trade that looks organized. Notebook logs and texts don't show how good you are.

Three things, done well

Pass it. Get paid for it. Get credit for it.

01

Pass the first inspection

Checklists built for your trade, with Pass / Fail / N/A, readings like test pressures and moisture, photos and signatures.

02

Get paid for extra work

Write the extra work ticket on site: labor, materials, photos. The GC's super signs on your phone, and the PDF goes straight to the GC.

03

Look like the pro you are

Daily logs, checklists, punch lists and quotes go out as clean PDFs in one tap, with a link to your public profile.

New · built in

Your crew writes in Spanish. The GC reads it in English.

Everyone picks the language they read. Daily logs, punch items and extra work tickets are translated for them automatically, and every PDF you send the GC comes out in English.
