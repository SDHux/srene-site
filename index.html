Where the name comes from

Solid. Reinforced. Equalized. No Extension.

Climbers use this checklist to judge whether an anchor is actually safe to trust with a life. We build every myBivy product against the same four principles, just applied to a business instead of a rope.

  1. PITCH 1 · S

    Solid

    A foundation that actually holds. Real persistence, real security, nothing built on a hope that the data's still there tomorrow.

  2. PITCH 2 · R

    Reinforced

    Nothing depends on one person remembering. If someone is out, the work still tells the whole story on its own.

  3. PITCH 3 · E

    Equalized

    You and your customer see the same shared picture. No one is quietly carrying more weight than they should.

  4. PITCH 4 · NE

    No Extension

    Nothing moves further than it's actually ready to. No shock-loading a customer who isn't there yet.
