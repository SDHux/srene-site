myBivy for Builders & Trades

Inspection checklist app for trade contractors

myBivy gives trade contractors ready-made inspection checklists for their trade (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, framing, drywall, concrete, roofing, flooring and painting) with Pass, Fail or N/A on every item, notes, photos, measured readings and signatures. Walk it before the inspector does and send the GC a clean PDF. Free for up to 10 people.

Start free (trade contractors)

What it does

  • Checklists for your trade: rough-in, top-out, final, startup, pre-pour and more, ready to use.
  • Pass, Fail or N/A on every item: with a note and a photo on anything that fails.
  • Readings, not just checkmarks: record test pressures, voltages, temperatures and moisture.
  • Add your own items: a checklist on a job can be extended for that job.
  • Signatures and a PDF: QC, GC and inspector sign-offs, sent as a clean report.

Questions builders and trades ask

Which inspection checklists are included?

Electrical rough-in, service and panel, and final; plumbing rough-in/top-out and final; HVAC rough-in and startup; framing; drywall; concrete pre-pour and pour log; roofing; flooring; painting; an all-trades rough-in; and a daily safety walk.

Can I print the checklists?

Yes. Every checklist is also a free printable page on srene.io.

Are the checklists the building code?

No. They're field checklists. Your inspector enforces the code and local amendments your jurisdiction has adopted.

Is the inspection checklist app free?

Yes, as part of myBivy for Trades, free for up to 10 people.
