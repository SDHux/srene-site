A Buildertrend alternative for small builders

Buildertrend is an all-in-one platform for residential builders and remodelers, covering sales, estimating, client selections, schedules and financials. myBivy is lighter and field-first: daily reports, plans and markups, punch items, crews and every trade on the job, with a free app for your trade contractors and English–Spanish translation built in. If the field side is where your days go, myBivy keeps it simple.

When Buildertrend is the better choice

If you want estimating, client selections and a homeowner portal in the same system, Buildertrend covers more of the office side. myBivy's client portal isn't available in the first release.

What small builders get with myBivy

  • Daily reports in minutes: crew, trades and weather filled in, photos, sign-off, PDF.
  • Plans and markups: the current revision on every phone, pins and clouds assigned to trades.
  • Every trade on every job: one tap to call or text, and a free app for your subs.
  • Works with no signal: save a job's plans and photos to the phone.
  • English and Spanish: reports, punch items, tickets and announcements translated for each reader.
