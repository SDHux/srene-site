myBivy for Builders & Trades

The construction app for small builders and the trades.

myBivy is construction management software built for small builders, GCs and trade contractors, not enterprise project teams. Daily reports, punch lists, T&M tickets, inspection checklists, plan markups, crews and timesheets live in one phone-first app that works with no signal and translates between English and Spanish. Trade contractors use it free; builders join early access at $89 a month.

Start free (trade contractors) Builders: join early access

What it does

Daily report appFile construction daily reports from the job site in minutes: crew and weather filled in, photos, work done, inspections, safety and sign-off, sent as a clean PDF.Punch list appA punch list app for builders and trade contractors: every item with a location, before and after photos and a due date, sent as a clean PDF. Free for trades.T&M ticket appWrite time and materials (T&M) tickets for extra work on your phone: labor, materials, equipment and photos, signed by the GC's super on site and sent as a PDF. Free for trades.Inspection checklist appReady-made inspection checklists for electrical, plumbing, HVAC, framing, drywall, concrete, roofing and more, with Pass/Fail/N/A, photos, readings and signatures. Free.Plan markup appKeep plan sets on every phone at the current revision, mark them up with pins and clouds, assign fixes to trades, and open saved plans with no signal.Crew timesheet appCrew time tracking for small builders and trade contractors: clock in to a job from the phone, hours by project, and a CSV export for payroll.Bilingual crews (English/Spanish)Your crew writes in Spanish, the office reads it in English. myBivy translates daily reports, punch items, extra work tickets and announcements for each reader.Quote app for tradesBuild quotes from a real bid form on your phone: scope, base bid, alternates, included and not included, schedule and terms, sent as a clean PDF. A won quote becomes a job.

How it compares

Procore alternativeProcore is built for mid-size and large GCs. myBivy is a lighter, phone-first app for small builders and the trades who work for them: flat pricing, free for trades.Buildertrend alternativeBuildertrend is an all-in-one platform for residential builders and remodelers. myBivy is a lighter, field-first app: daily reports, plans, punch and trades, free for subs.Fieldwire alternativeFieldwire is a field management app built around plans and tasks. myBivy adds daily reports, T&M tickets and trade checklists, a free trades app and English–Spanish translation.
