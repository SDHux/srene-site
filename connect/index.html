SRENE Connect · Free for trades and builders

Find the GCs and subs you should be working with.

A free portal for trade contractors and builders. Trades find general contractors and get on their bid lists; GCs find reliable subs. Keep a private pipeline, send a short intro campaign from your own email, and track the bids that come out of it.

One portal, both sides of the bid list

For trade contractorsGet on more GC bid lists.Find the general contractors building in your area, see who's busy, and introduce your company with a short email series sent from your own inbox.
For general contractorsFind reliable subs, by trade.Search licensed trade contractors by trade, state and county, build your bid list, and keep every conversation in one place.

How it works

  1. Find. Search a directory of general contractors and trade contractors in 21 states and DC, built from public license records and building permits. Filter by region, state, county and trade.
  2. Pipeline. Add the companies you want to work with, from the directory, by hand or from a spreadsheet, and track status, next steps, calls and notes.
  3. Reach out. A four-email intro campaign, filled in for each company and sent from your own email, one at a time or through a send queue. Opt-out lines and a do-not-contact list are built in.
  4. Accounts and bids. When a company engages, make it an account: track upcoming, open and past bids, with a reminder before each bid is due.

Win the bid, run the job in myBivy

Connect gets you on the bid list. When you win the work, run it in myBivy: myBivy for Trades (free) for checklists, extra-work tickets signed on site and daily logs, or myBivy for Builders for projects, daily reports, plans and every trade on the job.

Your data stays yours

  • Your contacts, notes, emails and bids are private to your company. SRENE only looks at them if you ask for help, to keep the service secure, or when the law requires it.
  • Campaign emails go out from your own email account, never from ours.
  • Company details you add to the directory (name, address, phone, website, trade) become part of SRENE's shared directory and stay there; your notes, contacts and emails never do. See the Terms.
  • Company listings come from public license records, building permits and our users. Want your company removed or kept from being contacted? Email us and it's done for every user.
