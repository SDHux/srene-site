The Deal Inspection Rubric

Ten questions you can ask about any deal without knowing the account. Every answer is verified, assumed, or unknown, because reps do not lose deals on the gaps they know about. They lose them on the things they were sure of that nobody ever confirmed.

Score a deal, save it, re-score weekly. Three answers carry a veto.

The Orchestration Log

A record of every internal person you directed, what you asked for, and what you did to get it. You have been leading without authority for years and have never once been able to describe it.

Start it at Enterprise and you have four quarters of it when you interview for the manager job.
