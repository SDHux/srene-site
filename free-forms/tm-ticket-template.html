Free T&M ticket template

A T&M (time and materials) ticket, also called an extra work ticket, records work done outside the contract: what was done and why it's extra, the labor hours, materials and equipment used, the markup, and a signature from the person who asked for it. What goes on a T&M ticket

  • Header: job, ticket number, date of work, customer or GC, who requested the work (by name), the location on site, and your company and foreman.
  • Work performed: what was done, and why it's extra.
  • Labor: each person or trade with hours, rate and amount.
  • Materials: description, quantity, unit cost and amount.
  • Equipment: each piece of equipment with hours, rate and amount.
  • Totals: subtotal, markup percent and dollars, and the ticket total.
  • Authorization: the name, signature and date of the person who authorized the work. Signing confirms it was requested and performed as described.

T&M ticket vs. change order

A change order changes the contract: scope, price or time, usually agreed and signed before the work starts. A T&M ticket documents extra work as it happens, when there's no time to price it first. Many contracts expect signed T&M tickets to be rolled into a change order afterward, so check what yours says.

How to get T&M tickets paid

  • Get the signature the same day, from the person who asked for the work. A ticket signed weeks later is easy to argue with.
  • Write down why it's extra: not on the plans, an owner request, another trade's damage, a field condition.
  • Use the rates and markup your contract allows.
  • Take before and after photos and attach them to the ticket.
  • Check your contract for who can authorize extra work and how soon notice is due.

Questions builders and trades ask

Who signs a T&M ticket?

Someone with authority to approve extra work for the customer: usually the GC's superintendent or project manager, or the owner on a residential job. Your contract should say who that is.

What markup should I put on a T&M ticket?

Whatever your contract allows for extra work. Many contracts set a percentage for overhead and profit; if yours doesn't, agree on it in writing before you do the work.

Is a T&M ticket the same as an extra work ticket?

Yes. Time and materials ticket, T&M ticket and extra work ticket are common names for the same form.

Does the Excel version calculate the totals?

Yes. It multiplies hours by rate and quantity by cost, totals labor, materials and equipment, and applies your markup to get the ticket total. It opens in Google Sheets too.
